# Clipboard Health

> Clipboard Health (now branded "Clipboard", clipboardworks.com) is a U.S. healthcare workforce marketplace connecting workplaces — long-term care, home health & hospice, hospitals, dental, and travel — with vetted independent professionals to fill open shifts, handling credentialing, scheduling, compliance, and instant pay. Backed by Initialized Capital and IVP. There is no public consumer API; the public developer surface is a set of open-source TypeScript utility libraries on npm.
